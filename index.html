How we review products

Every review follows the same method:

  • Check the label — does it contain clinically-studied ingredients at the doses used in studies?
  • Read the science — what do independent trials say, not what the brand's own marketing says?
  • Compare alternatives — cheaper or more effective options that do the same job
  • Flag the risks — side effects, interactions, and who should avoid it entirely
