Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Infinite Health Solutions ("we," "our," or "us") wants to be fully transparent with you about how this website makes money. This page explains our affiliate relationships in plain language.

What Is an Affiliate Relationship?

An affiliate relationship means we recommend a product or service and, if you click our link and make a purchase, the seller pays us a small commission. It does not change the price you pay — the commission comes out of the seller's share, not yours.

Our Affiliate Relationships

We participate in affiliate programs, which may include but are not limited to:

  • Amazon Services LLC Associates Program
  • ShareASale
  • Commission Junction (CJ Affiliate)
  • Impact
  • ClickBank
  • Direct brand partner programs

Any page containing a link to a participating merchant may earn us a commission if you click through and purchase.

How We Identify Affiliate Links

Affiliate links on this site are disclosed. Look for:

  • This disclosure page, linked in the footer of every page
  • An on-page note at the top of any review or recommendation article stating that we may earn a commission

Our Editorial Independence

Affiliate commissions never determine what we recommend. We only write about products and services we have researched or that align with our mission. If we receive a commission on an item, it does not change our assessment of its value, quality, or suitability.
