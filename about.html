About Us

Infinite Health Solutions is an independent health research and review site. Our job is simple: read the science, test what can be tested, and tell you the truth about whether a product is worth your money.

Why we exist

The health supplement and wellness space is full of exaggerated claims, paid endorsements, and ingredients that don't match the doses used in real studies. We started this site to cut through that — one product, one label, one verdict at a time.

What we will not do

  • Accept payment in exchange for a positive review
  • Let brands review or approve our content before publication
  • Claim that supplements cure, treat, or prevent disease
  • Recommend a product we wouldn't take ourselves

How we make money

Some links on this site are affiliate links, meaning we may earn a commission if you buy through them — at no extra cost to you. This keeps the site running. It does not influence our verdicts. Full details on our affiliate disclosure page.

Our standards

We only recommend products that (1) contain ingredients backed by independent research, (2) list those ingredients at clinically-meaningful doses, and (3) come from companies with reasonable quality controls. If a product fails any of those tests, we say so.
